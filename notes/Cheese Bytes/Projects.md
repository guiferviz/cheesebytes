A project starts as an inner pull I cannot ignore; sometimes a clear gap wanting
a solution, other times sheer curiosity about how something truly behaves
beneath the surface.

What I record here is the full journey: a motive that becomes a question, a
question unfolding into many ideas, ideas that lead to experiments, experiments
that grow into a system that slowly gains shape and structure. In the end, every
project yields distilled knowledge that makes the effort worthwhile.
