You just write a text, and [patorjk.com](https://patorjk.com/software/taag/)
converts it into a cool ASCII title. It has hundreds of styles. My favorite one?
[Calvin S](https://patorjk.com/software/taag/#p=display&f=Calvin%20S&t=Cheese%20Bytes).
Example:

```
╔═╗┬ ┬┌─┐┌─┐┌─┐┌─┐  ╔╗ ┬ ┬┌┬┐┌─┐┌─┐
║  ├─┤├┤ ├┤ └─┐├┤   ╠╩╗└┬┘ │ ├┤ └─┐
╚═╝┴ ┴└─┘└─┘└─┘└─┘  ╚═╝ ┴  ┴ └─┘└─┘
```
