Just as a mine can hold a full series about pathfinding, a procedural continent
can hold a series about geometry, map coloring, and fractal borders. In
[[Fantastic Lands]] we use a fictional world as the shared setting for three
core ideas: generating the base landmass, building Voronoi regions with a sweep
line, and breaking overly rigid borders with midpoint displacement.

For now this series is only a visual skeleton. The text, explanations, and
variants will come later.
