In the cheese cave, moving around is not always easy. Some paths are straight, while others seem to twist endlessly.  

But there are those who know the way: they know what to do at each crossing, which stones to avoid, and when to follow the light.  

**That is what an algorithm is**: a clear series of steps that, no matter how complex the labyrinth, will always lead you to where you want to go.
